# Map: ask-jeeves skill

## Destination

An `ask-jeeves` skill whose source lives in this repo at `skills/ask-jeeves/` (Grant, 2026-10-20). It is installed through `claude-global-settings`' `skillPackages` manifest, pinned to the deployed commit and symlinked into `~/.claude/skills/`, and invocable as `/ask-jeeves`. It passes every done-when test in the brief: the state-selection test, three depth memos on a sample offer letter, a caught fake citation, and the attorney finder on all four location options. It is backed by dated, deep-verified federal, Indiana and South Carolina research on the three priority fields. This map records each decision and lists the open items.

## Notes

- **Execution override.** This effort carries execution through to building, testing and installing the skill. That overrides wayfinder's plan-only default. The build, test and deploy tickets (18–20) are real work, not decisions.
- **Domain:** U.S. employment law, issue-spotting only (not legal advice). Law must be current as of **October 2026**. Flag anything that changed in 2025–2026.
- **Default jurisdiction:** federal law plus Indiana until Grant confirms otherwise. Built-in reference files cover federal, Indiana and South Carolina.
- **Priority fields:** (1) insider theft of IT assets, data and trade secrets; (2) restrictive covenants (non-competes, non-solicits, confidentiality); (3) employee contracts. "Insider theft" means employees or insiders taking trade secrets or data, not stolen devices or outside hacking.
- **Deep verification standard applies to design research.** Every source, case, statute and regulation is opened and confirmed real, and each claim is checked against it. Primary and authoritative sources are preferred, weak ones are flagged, and conflicts are noted. Anything unconfirmed is removed or marked UNVERIFIED. Each research file ends with a verification note. A separate verifier agent re-opens every source after the research agent finishes.
- **Quotes under 15 words.** Write page references as "pg. N".
- **HITL stops:** every grilling and prototype ticket goes to Grant, who answers for himself. Other stops: the pre-build summary (17) and the pre-deploy go (20). Research tickets run AFK.
- **Privacy:** this repo is **public**. Grant's home street address never appears in this repo, in URLs or in public queries. Searches use the city or ZIP only (Noblesville, IN 46060). The skill itself also lives here, in `skills/ask-jeeves/`, so it is public too.
- **Free to run:** no paid APIs or metered services. Use web search and fetch only.
- **Prior research:** `~/Developer/ai-solutions-llc-research-indiana-ip-law` and `~/Developer/ai-solutions-llc-research-attorney-shortlist` may be used as leads only. Re-verify everything and copy nothing in unverified.
- **Research record:** findings live in `research/` on main, kept as the design record. This overrides wayfinder's throwaway-branch default because Grant asked to keep the research files.
- **Skills to consult:** grilling and domain-modeling on HITL tickets, research on research tickets, prototype on the memo ticket, writing-for-agents and superpowers:writing-skills when building.
- **Glossary:** `CONTEXT.md`.

- [Skill structure and request flow](issues/08-skill-structure.md): input, then mode+depth in one round, then state selection ending in confirmation. Arguments pre-answer but never skip the state confirmation. References are per legal area with Federal/IN/SC sections and `as_of`/`verified` frontmatter; the memo prints the oldest as-of date. Quick is references-only, intermediate may use subagents when needed, deep uses one research subagent per area plus a verifier. A humanizer plain-language pass runs on the memo prose before it is shown, quotes and citations verbatim. Attorney finder is opt-in after the memo, run as a subagent. `disable-model-invocation: true`.
- [Live device location: what tool exists](issues/07-live-location-tool.md): neither Claude Code nor the desktop app has a location tool. Primary is a hand-built "Jeeves Location" Shortcut via `shortcuts run`, returning city and ZIP only, after AskUserQuestion consent and the Shortcuts Allow Once prompt. The fallback is ip-api, disclosed as approximate: plain HTTP, non-commercial. Typed entry is always offered. Untested: whether the prompt appears when the shortcut runs from the CLI.
- [Attorney-finder sources and feasibility](issues/06-attorney-finder-sources.md): the IN Roll and SC court lookups bar bots or need a browser, so the skill gives Grant the official site and name to check himself. Review sites are barred to bots, so evidence comes from search snippets plus pages Grant opens. FTC 16 CFR 465 supplies the fake-review signals. Budget sources: SC Bar LRS (≤$50 per 30 min), legal aid (125% FPL), RPC 1.2(c) limited scope.
- [Restrictive covenants: current law](issues/02-restrictive-covenants-law.md): the FTC non-compete rule is gone. Appeals were dropped 2025-09 and 16 CFR 910 was removed 2026-02-12, so enforcement is case by case (Gateway, Rollins, Adamas). NLRB GC 26-04 seeks to overrule *McLaren*. IN SEA 475 voids physician–hospital non-competes from 2025-07-01. SC has enacted nothing. 50 of 62 claims confirmed, no fabrications.
- [Insider theft: current law](issues/01-insider-theft-law.md): the DTSA, IUTSA and SC TSA are unchanged. *NRA Group v. Durenleau* (3d Cir., cert. denied 2026-03-30) says a policy breach without hacking isn't a CFAA violation. IN P.L.111-2026 adds phones to computer trespass (2026-07-01). All 61 authorities exist; 14 claims UNVERIFIED.
- [Employee contracts: current law](issues/03-employee-contracts-law.md): new 2026 arbitration law (*Flowers Foods*, *Jules*), and EFAA circuits holding one harassment claim takes out the whole case. EEOC harassment guidance rescinded. IN IC 22-5-9 (2026-07-01). SC *Lampo* and *Hess*. 104 of 129 claims confirmed, 21 corrected, no fabrications.
- [Choice of law, forum, and a 50-state covenant survey](issues/04-choice-of-law-and-state-survey.md): the 10-step primary/backup procedure is verified, with edits for the FL wage test, SC place of signing, and WA/OR/LA/TN reaching existing agreements. 25 jurisdictions changed in 2025–26, and 15 of those rows needed correcting. AL, AK, GA and MS are UNVERIFIED. Part B is a flagging aid: other states get researched at the chosen depth.
- [Adjacent fields of law: ranking](issues/05-adjacent-fields-ranking.md): proposed include: whistleblower, wage-hour, discrimination and retaliation, IP ownership, breach notice. Defer: NLRA, unemployment, severance tax, immigration. Exclude: AI as its own field, IN CDPA as employee privacy. iga.in.gov can't be read by WebFetch or curl, so IN Code official text comes from IN court opinions or in.gov agency pages.
- [Depth modes](issues/10-depth-modes.md): quick uses reference files only and is labeled unverified with the as-of date. It can't cover states without a reference file. Intermediate (the default) runs about 5–8 searches plus a fetch of every cited source; it uses subagents when the request touches 2 or more areas and needs more than 8 sources, or the state has no reference file. Deep uses one research subagent per area plus a separate verifier. If the search limit is hit, it falls back to fetching official URLs and marks the rest unverified.
- [Safeguards and refusals](issues/15-safeguards.md): a one-line disclaimer at the start and on every memo. An intake screen stops live matters (no memo, attorney finder only). The refusal list covers helping take or hide data in either mode. Citation rule: verified sources or ones opened this request only. Hire-an-attorney triggers include High risk, criminal exposure, uncertain state, an agency involved, or a deadline within 14 days. Privacy: no party names or contract text in queries, and home is stored as city and ZIP only.
- [Memo format](issues/12-memo-format.md): sections as in the prototype memo (employee, IN, intermediate). Risks table has 5 columns including Law [n], at most 6 rows and Low/Med/High likelihood. "In plain terms" lines are tagged by risk number. Recommendations are grouped Before signing / On the job / When leaving. Inline `[n, UNVERIFIED]`, and *(practical)* rows are allowed. 600–900 words of prose at every depth.
- [State-selection questions and recommendation](issues/09-state-selection.md): pre-fill from the input, then at most two short question rounds (work, home, employer, events; then clause, occupation and pay, signing date). Output is a candidate primary and backup with reasons, a 2–4 row differences table, and Clear or Uncertain with the reason in plain words. Confirmation is required before research, even when the state was passed as an argument. Built on the §A9 procedure.
- [Verification levels and fake-citation handling](issues/11-verification-levels.md): it checks Jeeves's cites **and** the input's cites. Quick marks input cites NOT CHECKED. Intermediate opens each source and spot-checks it. Deep re-opens every source, checks later history, tiers source quality and notes conflicts. A cite with no record anywhere is marked `[NOT FOUND: possibly fabricated]` and dropped. A fixed template for the verification note.
- [Creative solutions: how they're presented](issues/13-creative-solutions.md): 1–2 per memo, labeled "Creative, not standard practice," each with why it might work, Risk L/M/H, Enforceability Likely/Uncertain/Unlikely, and "run it past an attorney." It must pass a non-obvious test and stay lawful and within the safeguards.
- [Attorney-finder criteria](issues/14-attorney-finder-criteria.md): Grant set **≥4.3 stars** and **free or near-free**. Qualifying also takes ≥10 reviews across ≥2 sources with one in the last 24 months, plus a published free or ≤$50 consult, the bar LRS route, legal aid, or limited scope. Four location options, with a mismatch question. Grant checks license and discipline himself at the official lookup, and anyone unconfirmed is dropped. 3–5 ranked per location, with the not-an-endorsement note.
- [Adjacent fields: include, defer or exclude](issues/16-adjacent-fields-scope.md): Grant accepted the ranking as proposed.
- [End goal: ask it and let it steer the memo](issues/21-end-goal.md): Grant added this requirement. Jeeves asks for the end goal first. It steers the research and risk ranking, adds a Goal line, a reachability rating and a **Path to your goal** section to the memo, and aims every recommendation and creative option at it. It never overrides the safeguards.
- [Input document changes mid-run](issues/23-input-changes-mid-run.md): a new or replacement document triggers a re-scope. Jeeves re-reads it, re-runs the screen and the state pre-fill, then keeps or re-runs each area's research. The verification note gains an "Earlier input" line.
