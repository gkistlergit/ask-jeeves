## Question

What is the law, current as of October 2026, on employees or insiders taking trade secrets, data or IT assets, under federal law, Indiana and South Carolina? Cover civil and criminal claims, each element and remedy, what the employer must do to protect a trade secret, the DTSA whistleblower-immunity notice, computer-access statutes after Van Buren, conversion and treble-damage statutes, and preemption or displacement of other claims. List the facts that most often decide outcomes, for both the employer and the employee side. Flag every 2025–2026 change.
