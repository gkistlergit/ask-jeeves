# Adjacent fields: include, defer or exclude
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05

## Question

Given the ranking from the adjacent-fields research, which adjacent fields does the skill cover in built-in references, which does it flag only, and which does it exclude?

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-20. Grant accepted the recommendation, the ranking from [Adjacent fields of law: ranking](05-adjacent-fields-ranking.md):

- **Include (built-in coverage in `references/adjacent-fields.md`):** whistleblower protections (DTSA §1833(b) notice, SEC 21F-17, SOX); wage payment and wage-hour (IN and SC penalty and deduction rules); discrimination and retaliation (OWBPA, Speak Out Act); IP ownership; data-breach notification (narrow).
- **Defer (one-line flag in the memo's Adjacent issues, no analysis):** NLRA, unemployment eligibility, tax on severance, immigration.
- **Exclude:** workplace AI as its own field (its issues are handled under insider theft and IP); the Indiana CDPA as an employee-privacy law (it exempts employment data).
