## Question

What is the law, current as of October 2026, on offer letters and employment agreements under federal law, Indiana and South Carolina? Cover at-will status and how contracts alter it, IP and invention assignment (including any state limits on assigning employee-owned inventions), device, BYOD and return-of-property policies, wage-payment and deduction rules that affect clawbacks or device charges, arbitration and class waivers (including the EFAA), and severance and release requirements (OWBPA). Flag every 2025–2026 change.
