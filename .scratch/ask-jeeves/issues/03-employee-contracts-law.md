Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

What is the law, current as of October 2026, on offer letters and employment agreements under federal law, Indiana and South Carolina? Cover at-will status and how contracts alter it, IP and invention assignment (including any state limits on assigning employee-owned inventions), device, BYOD and return-of-property policies, wage-payment and deduction rules that affect clawbacks or device charges, arbitration and class waivers (including the EFAA), and severance and release requirements (OWBPA). Flag every 2025–2026 change.

## Answer

See `research/03-employee-contracts.md`. It went through research plus an independent verification pass on 2026-10-20: of 129 claims, 104 were confirmed, 21 corrected, 0 removed and 4 left UNVERIFIED. No fabricated authority was found.

- **Federal arbitration:** *Flowers Foods* (S. Ct., May 2026) widened the FAA §1 exemption but left open the independent-business and title-to-goods questions. *Jules* (May 2026) addresses jurisdiction over arbitral awards.
- **EFAA:** *Cornelius* (3d Cir. 2025) and *Memmer* (6th Cir. 2025) address when a dispute arises. *Bruce* (6th Cir. 2026) and *Ding* (9th Cir. 2026) take a whole case out of arbitration when it includes one harassment claim; both have dissents.
- **NLRB and EEOC:** GC 25-05 and GC 26-04. The quorum was restored Jan 2026. *Prime Communications* (Apr 2026) and *Ralphs Grocery* (375 NLRB No. 25, Aug 2026) were added by the verifier. The EEOC rescinded its harassment guidance (Jan 2026).
- **Indiana:** SEA 475 bans physician–hospital non-competes. IC 22-5-9 (from 2026-07-01) covers unauthorized-worker hiring and continued employment. IC 22-2-20 adds school-meeting leave.
- **South Carolina:** no statute changes. In *Lampo* (2025), silence on an opt-out is not assent to arbitrate. *Hess* (2025) trebled bonus damages without expressly holding that bonuses are "wages".
