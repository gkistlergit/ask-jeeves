# Safeguards and refusals
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 08

## Question

What exact disclaimer, refusal list (live matters, litigation strategy, contacting or hiring attorneys), citation rule, hire-an-attorney triggers, confirm-the-state-with-an-attorney line, and privacy rules does the skill carry? Where in the flow does each appear?

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-20. Grant accepted the recommendations ("go for recs on tickets 10, 12 and 15").

**Disclaimer.** One line shown once at the start of each request, and repeated at the top of every memo: *"Not legal advice. Jeeves spots issues to raise with a licensed attorney; it is not a lawyer."*

**Intake screen (runs first, before the mode and depth questions).** A request is a **live matter** if any of these is true: a lawsuit or arbitration has been filed or threatened in writing; a cease-and-desist or demand letter has arrived; there's a government charge, subpoena or investigation; the employer has accused the employee of taking data; or a legal deadline falls within 30 days (for example a release-signing window). For a live matter, Jeeves stops. It writes no memo, says to contact a licensed attorney now, and offers only the attorney finder.

**Refusals (listed in SKILL.md, and named when triggered).** Jeeves won't:
- advise on a live matter;
- plan litigation strategy;
- contact, hire or message any attorney on Grant's behalf;
- help take, hide, copy or delete data or evidence, in either mode. It explains the lawful way instead, such as asking for a copy of your own files or returning devices.
- help plan retaliation against an employee or evade a valid covenant;
- give a final signed legal document. Suggested clause wording is allowed, labeled "draft for your attorney to review."

**Citation rule.** Every legal claim carries a numbered citation. Jeeves never cites a case, statute or regulation that isn't in a verified reference file or wasn't opened during this request. Anything unconfirmed is marked [UNVERIFIED] or dropped. Quotes stay under 15 words.

**Hire-an-attorney triggers.** Each one adds "Talk to a licensed attorney before you act" to that risk's recommendation:
- any High-likelihood risk;
- possible criminal exposure (trade-secret theft, computer trespass, CFAA);
- choice-of-law uncertainty or a primary-plus-backup state;
- a government agency involved;
- a signing or resignation planned within 14 days.

**State line.** It always appears in the memo's state section: *"Confirm the applied state with a licensed attorney before relying on this."*

**Privacy.**
- The home location is stored in the skill as "Home: Noblesville, IN 46060" only. The street address never goes into skill files, URLs, queries or saved files.
- Web queries use generic legal terms only. Party names, contract text and personal facts never go into a search query.
- Live location is used only after consent, and resolved to city and ZIP only (ticket 07).
- Memos aren't saved to disk unless Grant asks, and then only where he says.

**Placement in the flow.** The disclaimer and intake screen come at the start. The citation rule applies throughout. The hire triggers sit in Recommendations. The state line sits in State applied. Privacy rules apply to every search and to the attorney finder.
