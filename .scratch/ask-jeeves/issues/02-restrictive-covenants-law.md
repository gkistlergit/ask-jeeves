## Question

What is the law, current as of October 2026, on non-competes, non-solicits (customer and employee), no-hire clauses, confidentiality and NDAs, and garden-leave clauses, under federal law, Indiana and South Carolina? Cover the status of the FTC non-compete rule and any FTC or NLRB activity in 2025–2026, reasonableness tests, blue-pencil versus reformation versus strict voiding, consideration rules (including mid-employment), industry-specific bans such as Indiana's physician rules, and tolling. Flag every 2025–2026 change.

## Answer

See `research/02-restrictive-covenants.md`. It went through research plus an independent verification pass on 2026-10-20: of 62 claims, 50 were confirmed, 6 corrected, 0 removed and 4 left UNVERIFIED, and 2 negative claims were not re-checked. No fabricated authority was found.

- **FTC rule is gone:** the FTC voted on 2025-09-05 to drop its appeals, and the Fifth Circuit dismissed them on 2025-09-08. 16 CFR 910 was removed on 2026-02-12 (91 FR 6507). Enforcement is now case by case: the Gateway order (C-4825) and Rollins order (C-4835), both 10-year orders, the Adamas no-hire order, an RFI and warning letters.
- **NLRB:** GC 25-05 rescinded the non-compete and *McLaren Macomb* memos. GC 26-04 (Aug 2026) asks the Board to overrule *McLaren* and *Stericycle*, but the Board still applied *McLaren* in April 2026.
- **Indiana:** SEA 475 (P.L. 207-2025, effective 2025-07-01) voids physician non-competes with hospitals, including stay-or-pay terms. No other 2025–26 non-compete law passed.
- **South Carolina:** nothing enacted. H. 4767 (a physician ban) passed the House and then stalled.
