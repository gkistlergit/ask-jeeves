## Question

How do courts decide which state's law governs an employment covenant or trade-secret dispute, current as of October 2026? Cover Restatement (Second) Conflict of Laws section 187 and section 196, how Indiana and South Carolina courts apply them, forum-selection clauses, and the states with statutes that void out-of-state choice of law or forum for employees (CA, MA, CO, WA, MN, others). Cover remote workers, where the employee lives versus where they work, and where events happened. Then give a 50-state plus DC table of non-compete status (banned, banned below a wage threshold, notice rules, limited), each row sourced and dated. What signals make the governing state uncertain?
