## Question

Which adjacent fields of law most often turn up in disputes over insider theft, restrictive covenants and employee contracts? Candidates include wage-hour, discrimination and retaliation, whistleblower protection, NLRA concerted activity, data privacy (including Indiana's consumer data law), workplace AI use, IP ownership, tax treatment of severance, immigration, and unemployment eligibility. Rank each as include (built-in coverage), defer (flag only) or exclude, with a reason and sources. Note what changed in 2025–2026.

## Answer

See `research/05-adjacent-fields.md`. It went through research, an independent verification (50 confirmed, 7 corrected, 0 removed, 14 UNVERIFIED) and an Indiana Code follow-up check (2026-10-20). No fabricated authority was found.

**Proposed ranking** (Grant decides in ticket 16):
- **Include:** whistleblower (DTSA §1833(b) notice, SEC 21F-17, SOX); wage payment and wage-hour (IN 2x, SC 3x, deduction rules); discrimination and retaliation (OWBPA, Speak Out Act; retaliation appeared in 61.6% of FY2025 EEOC charges); IP ownership; data-breach notification (narrow).
- **Defer (flag only):** NLRA; unemployment; tax treatment of severance; immigration (H-1B proclamation extended to 2027-09-21).
- **Exclude:** workplace AI as its own field (folded into insider theft and IP); the Indiana CDPA as an employee-privacy law, because it exempts employment data.

**Indiana Code access:** iga.in.gov serves only a JavaScript shell to WebFetch and to curl (checked 2026-10-20), and api.iga.in.gov returns 403. Official IC text has to come from Indiana court opinions that quote the section (`public.courts.in.gov/Decisions/api/Document/Opinion?Id=<id>`, a PDF) or from in.gov agency pages. Of the 11 IC sections cited, 5 are officially confirmed and 2 partly, 4 rest on secondary sources only, and 1 is UNVERIFIED.
