## Question

Which adjacent fields of law most often turn up in disputes over insider theft, restrictive covenants and employee contracts? Candidates include wage-hour, discrimination and retaliation, whistleblower protection, NLRA concerted activity, data privacy (including Indiana's consumer data law), workplace AI use, IP ownership, tax treatment of severance, immigration, and unemployment eligibility. Rank each as include (built-in coverage), defer (flag only) or exclude, with a reason and sources. Note what changed in 2025–2026.
