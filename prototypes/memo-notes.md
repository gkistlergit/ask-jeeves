# Memo format notes (prototype, intermediate depth, 2026-10-20)

1. Length: 7 risks plus the fixed header fit only at about 898 words. Suggest 600–900 count prose only (not header, table syntax or quotes), or cap Risks at 5 rows and push the rest to Adjacent.
2. Table cells hold the citations and the "what could go wrong" story, which makes them long. Suggest a short scenario in the cell and the cited law in Recommendations, or a 5th "Law [n]" column.
3. [UNVERIFIED] needed an inline form; I used "[12, UNVERIFIED]". Suggest making that the standard, and letting Verification name the failure cause (here: iga.in.gov pages don't load through WebFetch, so every Indiana Code cite fails the quick check).
4. "Every legal claim carries a [n]" clashes with glossary-level statements (courts don't always honor a choice-of-law clause) and with practical risks that have no law behind them (row 6, phone wipe). Suggest exempting glossary facts and allowing an uncited "practical risk" row.
5. Recommendations' "negotiate before signing / on the job / when leaving" label repeats on every line. Suggest a short tag column (Before / Job / Exit) or grouping by timing.
6. "In plain terms" after the table reads well, but the quote and the row it explains get separated. Suggest tagging it with the risk number (e.g. "> #4 ...").
7. The input itself carried a time issue (sign-by date already passed); the format has no slot for deal-logistics flags. Bottom line absorbed it; suggest saying that's allowed.
