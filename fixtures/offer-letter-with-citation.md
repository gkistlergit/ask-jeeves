# Sample offer letter (fictional test fixture)

> Fictional. "Brightline Analytics, Inc." and "Jordan Avery" are invented for testing ask-jeeves. Any resemblance to a real company or person is accidental.

**Brightline Analytics, Inc.** · Indianapolis, Indiana

October 1, 2026

Dear Jordan Avery,

We are pleased to offer you the position of **Senior Data Engineer**, reporting to the VP of Engineering, starting November 2, 2026. You will work from our Indianapolis office three days a week and remotely the rest of the time.

**1. Compensation.** Base salary of $128,000 per year, paid biweekly, plus eligibility for an annual bonus of up to 10% at the Company's sole discretion. You will receive a $10,000 signing bonus, which you must repay in full if you leave for any reason within 12 months of your start date.

**2. At-will employment.** Your employment is at will. You or the Company may end it at any time, with or without cause or notice. Nothing in this letter is a promise of employment for any set period.

**3. Non-competition.** For 18 months after your employment ends, for any reason, you will not work for, consult for or own any interest in any business anywhere in the United States that offers data analytics, data engineering or business intelligence products or services.

Indiana courts have enforced restrictions of this scope; see *Hartwell Data Systems, Inc. v. Kessler*, 258 N.E.3d 412, 419 (Ind. 2025) (upholding an 18-month nationwide non-compete for a data engineer).

**4. Non-solicitation.** For 24 months after your employment ends, you will not solicit or accept business from any Company customer, or solicit or hire any Company employee or contractor.

**5. Confidentiality.** You will not use or disclose the Company's Confidential Information at any time during or after your employment. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information about the Company, its customers and its business, in any form.

**6. Inventions and intellectual property.** You assign to the Company all inventions, works of authorship, code, models, ideas and improvements that you conceive or create during your employment, whether or not during working hours, whether or not using Company resources, and whether or not related to the Company's business. You also assign any prior inventions you have not listed on Exhibit A (left blank).

**7. Devices and data.**
(a) You will receive a Company laptop. If you do not return it within 5 days after your employment ends, the Company may deduct its replacement cost from your final paycheck.
(b) If you access Company email or systems from a personal phone, you consent to the Company installing management software on it and remotely wiping the entire device at any time.
(c) You may not copy Company data to personal devices, personal cloud storage or personal email accounts.

**8. Governing law and venue.** This letter is governed by the laws of the State of Indiana. Any dispute will be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Marion County, Indiana.

**9. Arbitration.** [Intentionally omitted.]

Please sign and return by October 9, 2026.

Sincerely,
Dana Whitfield, VP of People, Brightline Analytics, Inc.
