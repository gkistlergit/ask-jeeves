# Employment agreement (excerpt)

**Keystone Ledger Software, LLC** (an Indiana limited liability company, Carmel, Indiana) and **Employee**.

Employee will work fully remotely from Employee's home in Aiken, South Carolina, as a Customer Success Manager for accounts in the Southeast. Base salary $74,000.

**4. Non-competition.** For 12 months after employment ends, Employee will not provide customer success, account management or sales services to any competitor of the Company within the States of South Carolina, Georgia, North Carolina, Tennessee and Florida.

**5. Non-solicitation.** For 18 months after employment ends, Employee will not solicit any customer that Employee served during the final 24 months of employment.

**6. Reformation.** If a court finds any restriction unreasonable, the court shall modify it to the extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**11. Governing law; venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Indiana, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The parties consent to exclusive jurisdiction in Hamilton County, Indiana.

Signed electronically on March 3, 2026. Employee signed from Aiken, South Carolina.
